# App screenshots

Drop the app store screenshots here with these exact filenames — the showcase on
the homepage picks them up automatically (any that are missing are hidden, so you
can add fewer than four):

- `octolith-1.png`, `octolith-2.png`, `octolith-3.png`, `octolith-4.png`
- `octonium-1.png`, `octonium-2.png`, `octonium-3.png`, `octonium-4.png`

Portrait phone screenshots (roughly 9:19.5). PNG or JPG (keep the `.png`
filename, or update the `src=` in `index.html`). Aim for ≤ 400 KB each.

## Store links — done

Google Play (both apps) and the Octolith App Store link are wired in
`index.html`. Octonium isn't on the App Store yet, so its App Store badge shows
as "coming soon" (dimmed); fill `STORES.octonium.appstore` in `index.html` once
it's published.
